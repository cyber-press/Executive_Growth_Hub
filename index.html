Startup Guide

Start with the right foundation.

A strong business starts before the logo, website, or first sale. Use this section to move from a rough idea to a clear, testable, and organized business foundation.

Download Startup Checklist
💡 Before you launch: validate demand, define your offer, confirm basic registration needs, separate money tracking, and write a 90-day action plan.
01
1. Clarify the business idea
Define the exact problem you solve, who you help, and why your offer stands out.
🎯 Core Objective

Isolate a high-intent, painful market problem and establish a clear, defensible value proposition before investing capital.

📋 Action Checklist
  • Market Problem Validation: Identify an urgent, specific friction point that target clients are actively spending money to fix.
  • One-Sentence Value Proposition: Formulate a clear framework: "I help [Target Audience] achieve [Desired Outcome] through [Unique Architecture]."
  • Competitive Advantage Analysis: Isolate exactly why a buyer should select your platform over existing legacy solutions (e.g., pricing model, deployment speed, or tech integration).
  • Delivery Vehicle Definition: Clearly map your operational mechanics—determining if the solution will deploy as a digital download asset, a recurring B2B service package, or an automated SaaS application.
02
2. Understand your target audience
Identify ideal customers, urgent buying needs, habits, and key decision factors.
🎯 Core Objective

Identify ideal customers, urgent buying needs, habits, and key decision factors. The right audience makes marketing and sales easier because you speak directly to what they care about most.

📋 Action Checklist
  • Profile the customer: Identify their location, income level, daily habits, primary goals, pain points, and decision triggers.
  • Find where they gather: Locate their active channels: WhatsApp groups, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, trade events, or industry networks.
  • Study buying objections: List the top 3 reasons they might hesitate to buy and prepare trust-building answers.
03
3. Shape a high-converting core offer
Structure your delivery architecture to capture clear commercial interest.
🎯 Core Objective

Structure your delivery architecture and value deliverables to capture clear commercial interest.

📋 Action Checklist
  • Determine deliverables: Establish distinct pricing tiers, clear delivery milestones, and a fair value proposition aligned with target buyer brackets.
04
4. Build authority & brand credibility
Establish professional foundations to decrease trust gaps with early adopters.
🎯 Core Objective

Establish professional foundations, brand identity, and trust proof to decrease trust gaps with early adopters.

📋 Action Checklist
  • Establish brand assets: Lock down core brand colors, clean logo direction, a responsive website presence, and clear client trust proof parameters.
05
5. Set up money tracking & discipline
Lock down core administrative financial habits right at day one.
🎯 Core Objective

Lock down core administrative financial habits, dedicated accounts, and margin visibility from day one.

📋 Action Checklist
  • Maintain strict accounting: Separate your business banking entirely, record daily cash flows meticulously, and calculate accurate operational break-even points.
06
6. Execute a 90-day action plan
Organize your execution milestones sequentially to maintain high deployment speeds.
🎯 Core Objective

Organize your execution milestones sequentially to maintain high deployment speeds and avoid operational drag.

📋 Action Checklist
  • Execute sequential milestones: Structure monthly focus milestones to launch smoothly without getting overwhelmed by operational friction.

NG
Business registration in Nigeria (CAC)
Business names, company incorporation, and official registration pathways.

Most formal businesses in Nigeria register with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) to achieve legal recognition, corporate banking access, and commercial credibility.

  • Choose the right structure: Review the structures available through CAC and select the structure that fits the business.
  • Name search & reservation: Check availability and reserve your business name on the official CAC portal.
  • Complete pre-registration: Provide the required business, ownership, object, and registered-address information.
  • Upload required documents: Submit the identification and supporting documents requested by the official portal.
  • Pay official filing fees: Use the payment methods provided through the official registration process.
  • Retain registration records: Save the digital certificate and related registration documents for future business use.

US
U.S. business setup basics (SBA & IRS)
Entity selection, state registration, federal tax identity, and local requirements.

U.S. founders typically need to consider their business structure, state registration requirements, federal tax identity, and any applicable local permissions.

  • Choose an entity structure: Review common structures and the legal/tax implications that apply to your situation.
  • Check state requirements: Verify formation or registration requirements with the relevant state authority.
  • Review EIN requirements: Use the IRS to determine whether the business needs an Employer Identification Number.
  • Verify local requirements: Check city, county, licensing, zoning, or sales-tax obligations that may apply.
  • Separate business finances: Maintain appropriate financial records and dedicated business banking where applicable.

Tax identification, records, and tax-administration tools referenced in the EGH resource library.

Tax obligations depend on the business, location, structure, and activity. Use official tax channels and qualified professionals when needed.

  • Verify tax identification: Confirm the tax identifiers and registrations that apply to your business.
  • Maintain clean bookkeeping: Keep sales, expenses, invoices, receipts, and supporting records organized.
  • Use official filing channels: Verify current filing requirements, deadlines, and procedures directly with the relevant tax authority.
  • Seek professional review when needed: Tax treatment can differ by facts and jurisdiction.

Financial separation & bookkeeping discipline
Business records, operating accounts, receipts, and recurring financial review.

Clear financial records make it easier to understand cash flow, pricing, expenses, and the information you may need for tax or professional review.

  • Separate business activity: Keep business transactions distinguishable from personal spending.
  • Maintain transaction records: Use a repeatable process for revenue, expenses, supplier payments, and fees.
  • Archive supporting documents: Retain receipts, invoices, and other records in an organized system.
  • Review financial position regularly: Use your budget and pricing tools to understand operating costs and margins.
